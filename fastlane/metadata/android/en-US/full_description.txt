UltimateKeys is a modern keyboard that works entirely offline. There are no analytics, no ads and no cloud, and you can make it look exactly the way you want.

<b>Make it yours</b>
• Seven original built-in styles (Ultimate, Soft, Flat, Outline, Classic, Midnight, Paper), each with a light and a dark variant.
• A style editor with a live preview: key shape, gaps, borders, shadows, fonts, colors, gradients, popups, suggestion bar, bottom row and panels are all settings.
• Material You colors, and a warning when text would be hard to read.
• Export, import and share your styles as .ukstyle files.

<b>Type fast, in two languages</b>
• Spanish and English layouts (with ñ), symbols, numbers and an optional number row.
• Suggestions, auto-correction and next-word prediction that mix Spanish and English with no switching.
• Gesture typing: glide over the letters and the word appears.
• Accents and alternatives on long press, caps lock, cursor control on the space bar, drag on delete to select and remove.
• Your own dictionary, with import and export.

<b>Private by design</b>
• Private mode turns on by itself in incognito tabs and password fields, or by hand. While it is on nothing is learned and nothing is saved to the clipboard history.
• No analytics, no crash reporters, no ads, no accounts, no cloud.
• The "full" build does not even declare the internet permission. The "lite" build uses the network only to download a speech model when you ask for it.
• Clear everything the keyboard learned with one tap.

<b>Clipboard and emoji</b>
• Clipboard history that you control: keep it for an hour, a day, a week or forever, pin what you need, delete the rest. Items that apps mark as sensitive are never stored.
• Emoji by category, recent emoji, skin tones and search by name in English and Spanish, all offline.

<b>Dictation without a connection</b>
• Tap the microphone and speak: the text is recognized on your phone with Whisper (whisper.cpp), in Spanish or English.
• Audio stays in memory and is never written to disk or sent anywhere.
• Pick the model that suits your phone: a small one that is quick, or a larger one that is more accurate.

<b>Free software</b>
UltimateKeys is licensed under the GPL-3.0-or-later. It builds on the Android Open Source Project suggestion engine and word lists (Apache-2.0) and on whisper.cpp (MIT). Credits and licenses are listed in the app and in the source repository.
